Risk disclosures

Investments in private real estate and operating businesses involve a high degree of risk and are suitable only for investors who can bear the loss of their entire investment. Risks include, among others:

  • Loss of capital. Investors may lose some or all of the amount invested.
  • Illiquidity. Interests are generally not freely transferable, there is no public market, and holding periods may be long or extended.
  • No guaranteed returns. Projected or target outcomes are estimates based on assumptions that may not prove correct.
  • Development and construction risk. Delays, cost overruns, entitlement and permitting issues, and contractor or supply-chain problems may reduce returns.
  • Market and economic risk. Property values, rents, occupancy, interest rates and demand may change.
  • Operating business risk. Operating businesses are subject to competition, management, regulatory and consumer-demand risks.
  • Concentration. An investment in a single project or fund is not diversified.
  • Affiliated relationships and conflicts. Z-Co. and its affiliates may have interests that differ from those of investors; fees and conflicts will be described in each offering’s materials.
  • Oversight does not eliminate risk. Project review, reporting and oversight do not guarantee performance.

The complete list of risk factors will be set out in the offering materials for each opportunity.

What we collect. If you submit the investor inquiry form, we receive the information you enter: your name, firm, email, phone number (optional), investor type, indicative interest, area of interest, any message, and your confirmation of eligibility. We use it to respond to your inquiry and to determine eligibility.
